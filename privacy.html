1. Overview

HisabJod is an offline-first khata app for small businesses in India. All your data stays on your device. We do not use any cloud, login or server. No account required.

2. Data We Collect — None

We do not collect, store or transmit your business data to any server. All customers, transactions, reminders and settings are stored locally via localStorage on your device.

3. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Storage / FilesTo save backup JSON, export PDF/CSV to Documents and share receipt via WhatsApp. Files stay on device.
NotificationsOptional daily 9 AM reminder for overdue customers (via Local Notifications). You can disable in Settings.
ShareTo share receipt/ledger via system share sheet or WhatsApp (wa.me). No data sent to us.

4. WhatsApp

Sharing via WhatsApp uses the free https://wa.me deep link and system ACTION_SEND intent. Your message and PDF are sent directly to WhatsApp, not via our server. No WhatsApp Business API is used for automatic sending.

5. Ads (AdMob)

If ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization per Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out via Google Settings → Ads. Our ads.txt is at /ads.txt: google.com, pub-1607968585289432, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0

6. Backup & Restore

Backup creates a JSON file on your device. You control where to store it. Restore reads a file you select. No cloud backup.

7. Children

App is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the app's Settings → About - Privacy. Continued use means acceptance.
